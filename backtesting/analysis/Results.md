# Key Findings

The benchmark revealed a clear difference between Gold and Bitcoin: **the most useful signal source depends on the asset.**

* **Gold:** Trend-based signals consistently outperformed sentiment-based signals across the tested horizons. Sentiment alone performed around chance level and, at the 1-day horizon, underperformed the historical base rate. The combined signal also performed worse than the trend-only approach, suggesting that sentiment is currently adding noise rather than useful information to the Gold strategy.

* **Bitcoin:** The opposite pattern emerged. Trend-based signals were relatively weak, while sentiment-based signals were consistently the strongest of the tested approaches. The combined strategy largely followed the sentiment-only results, indicating that sentiment currently contributes more useful information for Bitcoin than trend alone.

### Overall Finding

The results suggest that **a single signal strategy should not be applied equally across different assets**. Gold appears to benefit more from trend information, while Bitcoin shows more potential from news sentiment.

Although many individual results do not reach a strong statistical significance threshold, the consistency of these patterns across multiple holding periods provides a useful direction for further development.

The next step is therefore not simply to make the signal rules more symmetric, but to investigate **asset-specific signal logic** and determine whether these observed differences remain consistent on additional data.
